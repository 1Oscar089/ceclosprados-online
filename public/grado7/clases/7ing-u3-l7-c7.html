📖 During: Theory of Possessive Adjectives

We use possessive adjectives to express possession and to say who the owner of something is.

Pronouns ➡️ Possessives

I ➡️ my
You ➡️ your
He ➡️ his
She ➡️ her
It ➡️ its
We ➡️ our
They ➡️ their

Examples

  • My name is Gabriela.
  • Your name is Nelson.
  • His name is Pablo. (Boy/Man)
  • Her name is Rosa. (Girl/Woman)
  • Its name is Firulais. (Pet/Thing)
