💡 Pre: Family Vocabulary Theory

To describe our family, we use specific nouns for each relationship. The family tree shows how family members are connected across generations.

Grammar Focus: Possessive 's

We use 's after a name to show possession or relationship.

  • Karla's grandfather.
  • Carlos's wife.

📖 During: Reading Comprehension Theory

Reading Tip: Scanning & True/False

When we read for information, we look for specific details (names, places, numbers). Compare the sentence carefully with the text. If even one detail doesn't match, the sentence is False (F).

🗣️ Post: Let's Guess!

To ask about age we use "How old is...?" and to confirm a relationship we use "I think... is your...".

A: How old is Carmen?
B: She is 60 years old.
A: I think Carmen is your aunt.
B: That's correct! Carmen is my aunt.
