Unit 3: Family Connections

Lesson 6 • English 7° • Karla's Family

Pre-Activity: Family Vocabulary Theory

To describe our family, we use specific nouns for each relationship. Family is generally divided into nuclear (parents and children) and extended (grandparents, uncles, cousins).

Grammar Focus: Possessive 's

We use 's to show possession.
Example: Karla's grandfather.
Example: Moises's wife.

During-Activity: Reading Comprehension Theory

When we read for understanding, we look for keywords. This text describes the origin and relatives of a character.

"My grandparents live in a small village in Chalatenango. My grandfather's name is Antonio and my grandmother's name is Amelia. They have six children. Their children's names are Juan, Vanesa, Gerber, Igor, Maria, and Ana. Gerber is married. His wife is Nora. They are my parents and we live in San Salvador. My grandparents have some farm animals such as cows, hens, roosters, and chickens. When we visit them, my sister and I play with the animals."

Read the paragraph and mark T (True) or F (False).

1. My grandfather lives in Chalatenango.
2. Amelia has four children.
3. My grandparents have six sons.
4. My mother is Nora.
5. I live in Chalatenango.
