⏱️ I. Activate: Inferring Adjectives

📓
Notebook Instruction: Copy the theory below in English and its translation. When finished, copy the correct sentences into your notebook.
  • In English, adjectives are NEVER pluralized. They don't take an 's' at the end. (e.g., expensive bracelets, expensives bracelets).
  • Adjectives do not have masculine or feminine distinction. (e.g., tall man / tall woman).
  • Exception: We use 'handsome' for men and 'beautiful' for women. Both mean attractive.

Choose the correct adjective based on the rules above.

  • 1. They have very cars.
  • 2. My mother is a woman.
  • 3. My father is a man.
  • 4. I bought two cell phones.
  • 5. We live in houses.
