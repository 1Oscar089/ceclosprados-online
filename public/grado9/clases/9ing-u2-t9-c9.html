During: Conversation Time!

Listen to the conversation and repeat each line to practice. Pay attention to the dates.

Eduardo: Hi, Andrea! I heard that your birthday was yesterday.

Andrea: Good afternoon, Eduardo. Yes, April 16th is my birthday. In fact, I was born on 04/16/2010.

Eduardo: Happy belated birthday!

Andrea: Thank you so much! What about you, Eduardo? When were you born?

Eduardo: I was born on 12/27/2011.

Andrea: Oh! It's very close to Christmas.

Eduardo: Yes, it is. Well, I have to go. Glad to see you!

Andrea: Take care, Eduardo!
