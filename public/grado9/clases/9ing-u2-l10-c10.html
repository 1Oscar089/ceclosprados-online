⏱️ I. Activate: Simple Past Questions

📓
Notebook Instruction: Copy the grammar structure and its translations. When finishing the exercise, copy the correct sentences into your notebook.
To make Yes/No questions in Simple Past, we ALWAYS use the auxiliary verb 'Did' at the beginning (except with the verb to be).

Important! Because we are already using 'Did', the main verb goes in its BASE FORM (not in past tense).
Auxiliary Verb Subject Verb (base form) Complement ?
Did you go to the beach ?
Short Answers
Yes, I / you / he / she / they did.
No, I / you / he / she / they didn't. (did not)
