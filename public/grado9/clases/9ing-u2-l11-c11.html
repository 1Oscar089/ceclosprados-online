📖 II. During: Information Questions

📓
Notebook Instruction: Draw the Wh- Words cards in your notebook. Then unscramble the sentences and copy them in order.
Wh- words help us to get specific information. To form these questions, we simply place the Wh- word before the auxiliary 'Did'.

What

for things

When

for time or dates

Why

for reasons

Who

for people

Where

for places

How

for manner
